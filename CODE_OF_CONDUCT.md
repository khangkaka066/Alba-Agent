# Code of Conduct

We want NexusAgent to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their background or experience. This code is a short summary in the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our standard

Expected behaviour:

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give and accept constructive feedback; criticise ideas, not people.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Credit other people's work.

Unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, insults or sexualised language and imagery.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or publishing someone's private information.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and contributions that break this code, and may restrict participation of anyone who does. To report a problem, contact the maintainers through a private [security advisory](https://github.com/khangkaka066/Nexus-Agent/security/advisories/new) or the contact details on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports are handled in confidence.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project in public.
